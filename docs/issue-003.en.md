## Evidence and counterevidence

WSJ's publicly accessible opening reported removal preparations; Bloomberg's readable syndicated report described an outside succession search. These are more specific than general speculation, but anonymous sources may overlap; no mandate, board resolution or firm timetable was obtained. Reporting alone is not a qualifying announcement.

Huang's strategic importance could encourage retention of his influence or continued CEO tenure; it is not a veto on changing the title. Senator Cramer's public resignation call establishes political pressure. US-China rivalry and China-hawk motives remain creator hypotheses, with no direct evidence of orchestrated disclosures. Further relationship reporting remains subject to allegation/access limitations.

The strongest countercase was prior board support, an open investigation, time needed for succession, possible contingency planning, negotiation or genuinely temporary arrangements. Those possibilities were not eliminated by media reporting.

## Method and uncertainty

AI reviewed evidence and assigned judgmental scenario/path parameters. 70% relied on a strong inferred exit intention and fairly rapid conversion to an announcement. 60% reduced reliance on those unobserved internal states. The native subjective-evidence/logit diagnostic was about 28.65% in v3 and 31.81% in v4, a substantial unresolved disagreement rather than independent validation. All lacked matched historical calibration. Arithmetic checks, AI authorship and one directional match do not prove accuracy. Prior numerical market exposure prevents a pristine-blind claim.

## What was confirmed afterward?

NVIDIA announced Huang's resignation as president, CEO and director, effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO while retaining CFO, and the company is searching for a permanent successor. Huang was not merely on leave retaining CEO.

Official RSS publication metadata is November 12 21:48 Beijing; SEC public acceptance is 21:52:41. Both local freezes precede those verified records, not proof of the earliest disclosure across all channels. The 8-K also says the board accepted the resignation on November 11, effective immediately: effective date and public disclosure are different. This cannot be promoted as predicting a secret decision already made or the exact departure time.

The definitive announcement is consistent with the question's primary YES announcement condition. Original freezes/ledger are unchanged; Polymarket final settlement was not checked, no hit-rate/Brier claim computed, and political motives are not established.
