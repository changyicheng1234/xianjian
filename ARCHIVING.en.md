# Archiving policy

Use separate episode directories with reports, sources, frozen estimates, methods and limitations. Store estimates in files, not only editable release notes. Numbered releases use immutability; corrections get new versions with reasons. Keep post-event reviews separate and preserve failures as well as successes.

Local times, hashes and commit times alone do not prove first public availability. Check actual release published_at and platform records.

Record author, original date, retrieval date and reading scope. Distinguish excerpts from full reading. Exclude credentials, internal sessions and full third-party article/HTML/PDF bodies. Issues should identify episode, file, original source and proposed correction.

Use templates/ and episodes/index.json for later episodes and website integration. Preserve incomplete-review labels.
