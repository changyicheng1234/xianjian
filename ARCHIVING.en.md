# Archiving policy

Future episodes default to three core public materials:

1. **A short Chinese article**, readable directly on GitHub: question, probabilities,3–5 main reasons, strongest countercase and limits, with an accessible method explanation. PDF is optional.
2. **Key sources inline in the article**, preferably5–10 original links, dates and one-line relevance notes covering support and counterevidence. Do not export every collected source by default.
3. **A frozen prediction record** with question, resolution conditions, probabilities, data cutoff and freeze time. If the video compares a market, include its quote/time/type in the report; a complete orderbook is not required.

Keep complete datasets, computation code, row-level predictions, detailed diagnostics, internal logs and full research archives local by default. Prepare technical appendices separately when needed; more files are not a substitute for clarity.

Normally an article with sources plus a frozen JSON is sufficient. PDFs, ZIPs and illustrations are optional. The homepage indexes years, months and episode numbers linking to docs/issue-NNN.md for direct reading; downloads belong in a collapsed section at the end.

Use separate episode directories and a short README. Numbered immutable releases preserve snapshots; corrections and post-event reviews remain separate, including failures. Do not remove or replace Episode002's existing full archive. Apply this minimal policy to subsequent episodes.

Local times, hashes and commit times alone do not prove first public availability. Check actual release published_at and platform records.

Retain relevant source dates and reading scope, distinguishing excerpts from full reading. A smaller package must still disclose the strongest countercase, material gaps and revisions. Exclude credentials, internal sessions and full third-party article/HTML/PDF bodies. Issues should identify episode, file, source and correction.

Use templates/ and episodes/index.json for later episodes and website integration. Preserve incomplete-review labels.
